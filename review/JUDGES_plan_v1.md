## Lens 2

Verdict: The plan follows the author's order (setting → pairs → multimodal → regression collapse + sim → flow matching slow, jerky, why → videos → one step / 3.67x / 11x → manifold → cIMLE → sim) and a viewer would follow it; the technical faults are local: clip 1 says a chunk is "one forward pass" before clip 3 says ten, clip 3's invertibility captions say "probability" where only density is non-zero and blame jerk on off-manifold samples alone (the paper blames replans), and clip 4.4's right caption makes an unsupported "like flow matching" claim at 22 words.

- **[should-fix] clip clip 3, 3.2 caption 3** — A Gaussian has non-zero DENSITY everywhere; the probability of any point is zero. 'Probability' is the wrong quantity and a reviewer will read it as a mistake.
  Fix: The Gaussian noise has non-zero density everywhere in the ambient space.
- **[should-fix] clip clip 3, 3.2 caption 4** — Same 'probability' error, and with branch (1) dropped the step needs the map to be a bijection of the whole space (onto), not merely invertible: an injective map into a higher-dimensional space has a measure-zero range. For a diffeomorphism of R^n with a full-support base density the pushforward density is p_z(T^-1(x))|det J| > 0 everywhere, so the statement is true once phrased as density + bijection.
  Fix: The map is a bijection, so the sample density is non-zero everywhere too.
- **[should-fix] clip clip 3, 3.2 caption 5** — 17 words (over the ~14 limit) and 'cannot lie on' is stronger than what full support gives: samples hit a measure-zero manifold with probability zero; training can concentrate density arbitrarily close but not put mass on it.

- **[should-fix] clip clip 3, 3.2 caption 6** — "Off-manifold actions make the motion jerky" states a sole cause the paper never gives.
